# Motivation

Two minds can hold the same propositions and appear identical from the outside—they give the same answers to the same questions. Yet in one mind, those propositions sit as a pile of disconnected, isolated facts (A). In the other, they can all be derived from a few core truths (B), so to that mind the facts are obviously connected. That connection is understanding. Understanding preserves knowledge—because its connections hold that knowledge in place—compresses it, and is simply better.

- Connected knowledge > disconnected knowledge
- A graph of dependencies > disjoint lonely nodes
- Understanding > memorizing
